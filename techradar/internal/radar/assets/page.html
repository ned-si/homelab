Tech radar

What the homelab is built with, and why.

Every technology and technique the homelab uses, tries, keeps an eye on or decided against, each with a one-line reason and a link to where the decision is written down. Those reasons used to be spread over docs, specs and issues. Now they are on one page, in the spirit of Zalando's tech radar (minus the JavaScript).

  • Adopt: in use, and I would choose it again.
  • Trial: in use on trial, or with a known limit I am working on.
  • Assess: researched or planned, not in use yet.
  • Hold: decided against, removed or replaced; the reason says why.
