# 2026-11-15: NAS hung after a power cut

## Summary

A short power cut at about 21:24Z rebooted the four nodes, which came back
on their own within two minutes. The NAS stayed powered but stopped at the
power dip: no network, no logs, for 9 h 38 min, until the operator
power-cycled it the next morning. Every app with a volume, Keycloak (and so
every app behind single sign-on) and the main Prometheus waited on storage
the whole time. No alert paged: the paging rule ran in the Prometheus that
was itself waiting on the NAS.

## Impact

From 21:27Z the cluster ran without storage. Pods with iSCSI or NFS volumes
stayed `Unknown` or `ContainerCreating`. Keycloak's database is on iSCSI, so
Keycloak and the `*-sso` proxies crash-looped and no SSO login worked. The
nightly backups and other CronJobs of the night failed with
`DeadlineExceeded` or `BackoffLimitExceeded`, and the Argo CD hook Job
`signoz-telemetrystore-migrator` failed at 22:36Z. The household could not
reach the apps until about 07:12Z.

## Cause

Confirmed: a power cut. The nodes have no UPS and rebooted cleanly. The NAS
has no UPS either and did not come back on the network.

What the NAS logs show (`/var/log/messages` and the alert list, read
through the TrueNAS API after the reboot):

- The syslog runs at a steady 240 lines per hour all day, then stops at
  21:24:07Z and resumes with the boot banner at 07:02:57Z. There is no
  shutdown, panic, watchdog or link-state line in between. A NIC or cable
  fault alone would have logged `igb0: link state changed to DOWN` and the
  later up.
- TrueNAS raised `UnscheduledReboot` for the 07:02Z boot only, none for
  2026-11-15: the OS did not boot after the cut.
- `/var/crash` holds no dump.

Suspected: the NAS froze at the power dip, or lost power, came back and hung
before the OS started (POST or boot loader). Without a display on the NAS,
which of the two is not known. The operator found it on, with every cable in,
so the BIOS power-restore setting was not the cause this time. The NAS has
rebooted unexpectedly eight more times since 2026-11-03, mostly at night.

Why it did not recover or page:

- Nothing can power-cycle the NAS remotely: no BMC, no smart plug, and a
  frozen TrueNAS cannot reset itself.
- `HomelabNASDown` ran in the main Prometheus, whose TSDB is on an iSCSI
  volume. That Prometheus could not start, so the alert could not fire.
- The healthchecks.io heartbeat stops when Prometheus does, but nobody
  checked whether it notified.

## What went well

- The nodes, etcd, the Gateway and Argo CD came back without help within two
  minutes of the power.
- Once the NAS answered, every volume reattached and the stateful apps
  recovered within ten minutes without a restore.
- The pool and the data were intact.

## What went badly

- Nine and a half hours of outage with no page.
- What has no retry stayed broken after the NAS came back: the failed
  CronJob Jobs (`KubeJobFailed`, Argo CD `Degraded`), the failed Argo CD hook
  Job and the SSO proxies' long crash-loop back-off. They needed a person.

## Where we got lucky

- The last good backups were about 25 hours old when the failed jobs were
  re-run, and the pool came back healthy.
