What this is

My corner of the web: the projects I work on and what I write about them. No newsletter, no tracking.

What you can expect

Mostly security, reliability and platform engineering, with a soft spot for developer experience: the friction we often overlook, from a permission prompt to the keyboard under your fingers. I write when I have something worth saying, which is to say: not every week.

What is here so far:

  • Blog: how the homelab is built and run, the trade-offs behind it, and what I learn on the way.
  • Homelab dashboard: the homelab in numbers, live: availability, the cluster, DORA metrics, security.
  • Tech radar: what the homelab uses, tries, assesses and holds, and why.
  • About: who I am, what I have done so far, certifications and talks.
  • Projects on GitHub: the homelab, the keyboard firmware and the drum charts, all in the open.
