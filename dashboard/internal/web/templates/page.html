DORA metrics

DORA, short for DevOps Research and Assessment, is the research programme behind the State of DevOps reports. It sums up software delivery in four metrics: how often changes reach production (deployment frequency), how long a change takes to get there (lead time for changes), how often a change breaks something (change failure rate) and how quickly things are back to normal when it does (time to restore).

Each metric falls in one of four bands, Elite, High, Medium or Low, taken from the performance clusters of DORA's 2023 report. Of course, the metrics were made for teams, not for one person and a few agents, but they make a fair yardstick all the same.

{{- template "tile" .DORALevel}}

The overall level is the lowest of the four bands, so one weak metric is never averaged away.

{{- range .DORA}}{{template "tile" .}}{{end}}

Rework rate counts the unplanned deploys: rollbacks, fix forwards, and fix merges made during an incident or in the 24 hours after it, over all deploys. Rework rate, DORA's fifth metric since 2024, has no band here: the 2023 clusters these bands come from predate it.
