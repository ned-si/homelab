This is the homelab in numbers. A small job reads the cluster's own
monitoring and the repository once a night, then hands this page the
figures as a static file. The page itself has no access to anything, which
is rather the point.

Why once a day, and always for yesterday? A live status page would tell
anyone watching when the house is down, and a power cut is precisely when
you'd rather nobody knew. A finished day says nothing of the sort.

Not every app is here. The download tooling is left out on purpose (sorry,
nothing to see there).
